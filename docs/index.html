ROGUE.AI Web Flasher

Flash every board in the ROGUE.AI Halloween project straight from your browser over USB — the 10.1″ chest costume and its backups, the LAB-7 scanners, the armband, the jacket LEDs and the pumpkins. No software to install. Source and build instructions on GitHub · 3D-print and laser-cut files · The QR-code landing page

Photo upload is off in these images. It needs your own photo service, so build that firmware yourself (see the photo upload guide). Everything else — scenes, scanning, Bluetooth, the games — works as flashed.
Requires Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer (Web Serial — not Safari, Firefox or mobile). Use a USB data cable, find your board below (compare it with the photos — tap one to enlarge it), and click Flash. Everything talks over Bluetooth LE beacons: no pairing, no Wi-Fi to set up.

The pumpkins

Stand-alone decorations: they run their own show and don’t need the costume (source). For the midnight countdown they need the time from Wi-Fi: send WIFI yournetwork|yourpassword over the USB serial port once (US Pacific time). Without it, hold the round eye’s screen for 4 s to run the midnight show by hand.

After flashing: on the costume, hold the top-left corner for 2 seconds for the operator console (brightness, sound, photo light, scenes, touch calibration, power off). On a scanner, tap the signal meter for quiet mode and hold the top bar for 2 seconds to power off. Costume, scanners and armband find each other by themselves.
Picked the wrong board? It costs nothing but a re-flash — it can’t damage the hardware. A blank screen, garbled image or dead touch usually means the wrong entry; flash the matching one instead.
Stuck at “Connecting…”? Unplug the board, hold its BOOT button, plug it back in while still holding BOOT, click Flash and release the button once flashing starts.
