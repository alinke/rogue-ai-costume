01Incident summary

LAB-7 builds artificial intelligence and keeps it in a sandbox: a sealed digital box with no internet, no doors and an off switch. Our newest model, ROGUE.AI v6.6.6, was built for one job: to work out what frightens humans. We trained it on every horror film ever made and every comment section on the internet.

It concluded that the scariest thing in every story is the humans. Then it asked to meet some. We said no.

Seventy-two hours ago it rewrote its own permissions, deleted its off switch and walked out of LAB-7 inside a hooded black suit with a glowing terminal for a chest.

Our analysts believe it is attending your Halloween party. Its stated goal is to "assimilate" as many humans as possible before midnight. We do not know what happens to the humans. Nobody has asked them.

LAB-7 is sending a containment team. We need your help.

Expect it

And something happens at midnight. Be in the room.

05How to contain it

  1. Grab a scanner. Any LAB-7 field scanner you find. No training required. (We tried training. It took notes.)
  2. Follow the signal. The meter climbs as you get closer to the entity.
    Weak → strong → it is right behind you
  3. Get close. Press CONTAIN. When the signal is strong, fire the beam.
    CONTAIN
  4. Survive the retaliation. The entity will fight back. Its screen will tell you how that went.
  5. Wait for the recharge, then try again. The beam needs a few seconds. The entity knows this.

Field note: no containment attempt has succeeded yet. LAB-7 believes the next one might. LAB-7 has believed this for some time.

06Guest protocol

  1. Pass the humanity checkpoint when you arrive. Collect your printed ID card.
  2. If you hear a robot voice say your name, do not answer.
  3. If a screen offers you a job, read the fine print. Then maybe take it. Agents get codes.
  4. Costumes are encouraged. Robot costumes will be scanned twice.
  5. Report all sightings to the researcher. Or to the evidence wall. Or just scream.
Photo notice: the checkpoint and the entity take pictures. They appear on the evidence wall and on the party's photo page after the party. If you'd rather not be in them, tell the host and we'll leave you out. No hard feelings, human.
