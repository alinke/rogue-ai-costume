LAB-7 app: privacy policy

Last updated October 24, 2026

LAB-7 is a party game for iPhone: a scanner that tracks a costumed "rogue AI" over Bluetooth, a "humanity test" that turns a photo into a joke ID card, and an evidence wall for the party. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and nothing leaves your phone unless you join a party's event.

Without joining an event

  • Camera: used only for the humanity test. The ID card is saved on your phone, in the app's folder (Files app › On My iPhone › LAB-7). It is not uploaded.
  • Bluetooth: used to detect the costume's signal and send it the CONTAIN beam. The app only reads the costume's own broadcast; it does not connect to or identify other devices.
  • Settings and scores (your agent name, number of CONTAIN shots and tests) stay on your phone.

When you join an event

A party host can invite you to their event with a link or QR code. The app shows what joining does and asks you to agree before anything is sent. While you are joined:

  • ID cards from your humanity tests are uploaded to the host's evidence wall, where everyone at the event, and anyone with the wall's link, can see them. Only photograph people who are happy to be on it.
  • Leaderboard: the agent name you choose and your counts of CONTAIN shots and humanity tests are sent for the event's TOP AGENTS board.
  • A random device ID, created when the app is installed and not linked to you, your Apple ID or your phone's hardware, is sent with uploads to prevent spam and keep your leaderboard entry.
  • The event's photos are stored by the host's photo service (Google Cloud Storage) and are deleted when the host clears the wall after the event.

You can leave an event at any time on the AGENT tab; nothing more is uploaded after that.

Reporting and hiding photos

Anyone can report a photo on the evidence wall: it is hidden for everyone right away and reviewed by the host. You can also hide every photo taken by a particular phone; that only affects your own app.

What the app never does

  • No accounts, no email, no contacts, no location, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking across apps or websites, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Notifications

If you allow it, the app schedules a reminder on your phone for the event's "midnight protocol". It is a local notification: no push server is involved.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.
